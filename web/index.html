01THE CHALLENGE

A distant world.
No easy choices.

On Mars, a small crew depends on the systems that keep them breathing, fed and protected. Power is limited, and a storm can change the plan.

Mission Trainer turns those trade-offs into a hands-on learning challenge: decide how to share power, then see how the mission responds.
