02THE CHALLENGE

A distant world.
No easy choices.

A Mars crew would face hard limits: supplies, energy, radiation and a long distance from Earth. A storm can complicate the plan, while communication delay makes timely decisions matter.

Mission Trainer models a small subset of those pressures. The playable scenario focuses on power allocation, resource levels and selected storm events; other challenges are real mission context, not mechanics in this build.

05SURVIVAL SYSTEMS

THE SIMULATION'S FOUR ALLOCATIONS

Keep the base
in balance.

The scenario turns a complex mission into a focused power-allocation challenge. These are simplified game rules, not engineering specifications for a real habitat.

SCENARIO POWER 10 UNITS / SOL

A dust-storm event can reduce the available solar power for that sol.

ILLUSTRATIVE GAME RULE
01

Life support

Power allocation affects the scenario's air-and-water resource. The baseline upkeep is 3 units per sol.

AIR / WATER
02

Greenhouse

The greenhouse allocation changes the food resource. Its scenario baseline is 2 power units per sol.

FOOD
03

Radiation shield

Shield allocation reduces the dose added by a sol in the simulation. The game caps that reduction at 75%.

DOSE / GAME POINTS
04

Science

Power assigned to science earns scenario points toward the 24-point mission goal.

GOAL / 24 POINTS
+

Weather Station

An optional scenario decision: it uses 1 power unit per sol and can provide a storm warning one sol ahead.

OPTIONAL / 1 UNIT

Resource and dose values are game abstractions. They do not represent live conditions or direct real-world operating limits.

08AI COMMANDER

MEET COMMANDER NOVA

Science, translated
into mission guidance.

Commander Nova is the game's mission-guidance voice, designed to explain a briefing or sol result and offer a practical tip. The project contains an API-backed commander service and bundled fallback lines.

The checked-in game configuration leaves the API base URL empty. Live Gemini responses are not confirmed for this build; bundled local fallback text is the configured path. AI tools also assisted with project code and documentation; the team's final disclosure can be added here.
